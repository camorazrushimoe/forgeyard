# Triage

Label the issue as `bug` or `enhancement` (or say why neither).
State:

- `needs-info` — cannot work yet
- `ready-for-spec` — needs to-spec
- `ready-for-review` — spec exists, needs adversarial-review
- `ready-for-agent` — spec accepted
- `wontfix` — with a reason

Comment the classification and the next legal step. Do not implement.
